# Mushukistan Content Policy

Version: 2026-08-01

Mushukistan hosts user-generated cat sightings, cat photos, cat profiles, descriptions, comments, likes, reports, and lost-pet posts.

Mushukistan may remove or hide content that violates law, these policies, user privacy, animal safety, platform rules, or service security.

Prohibited content includes:
- Illegal content.
- Child sexual abuse or exploitation.
- Hate, harassment, threats, or targeted abuse.
- Private personal data without consent.
- Sexual content.
- Spam, scams, malware, or platform manipulation.
- Copyright or trademark infringement.
- Content encouraging animal cruelty.

Reports are reviewed within 7 days. Urgent safety, illegal-content, privacy, or animal-cruelty reports may be prioritized when feasible.
