# Mushukistan Community Guidelines

Version: 2026-08-01

Use Mushukistan to help street cats and lost pets in Tashkent.

Allowed:
- Real cat sightings and lost-pet posts.
- Helpful comments.
- Photos and descriptions relevant to cat welfare.
- Accurate map locations when safe to publish.

Not allowed:
- Harassment, threats, hate, abuse, or doxxing.
- Animal cruelty promotion or graphic shock content.
- Sexual content.
- Spam, scams, malware, or unrelated advertising.
- Publishing private personal data without permission.
- Copyright-infringing photos or text.
- False reports or impersonation.

Avoid showing people, homes, license plates, private yards, or sensitive places unless you have permission.

Use the report tools when content appears unsafe, illegal, abusive, or privacy-invasive.
